# Game Design Document (GDD) — LokSabha Legends: The Great Kursi Showdown

## 1. Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Select & Strategize**: Choose from 10 distinct satirical caricature fighters (or unlock the Boss challenge against Shanti Bapu) and pick one of 3 iconic Indian stages.
2. **Battle for the Kursi (Best of 3 Rounds)**: Engage in fast-paced 60-second 1v1 rounds using spacing, target combos, directional specials, guard-breaking grabs, and Shakti Super moves while the live TV News Desk roasts every hit.
3. **Read the Headlines & Progress**: Review the post-match *Daily Jumla* newspaper front page, earn verified XP and Kursi Coins, unlock achievements and titles, and climb the global ELO leaderboard.

## 2. Playable Fighter Roster (Original Satirical Archetypes)
1. **Vikas Mantri (MitraMan)** — *All-Rounder*: Balanced strikes, "56-Inch Chest" guard poise, Super: *Masterstroke Laser Wave*.
2. **Yatra Veer (Marathon Boy)** — *Rushdown Speedster*: Fastest walk/dash speed, rapid target combos, Super: *Kanyakumari-to-Kashmir Sprint Blitz*.
3. **Bengal Tigress (Didi Express)** — *Counter Brawler*: High-priority low sweeps (`Chappal Sweep`), Super: *Khela Hobe Hurricane*.
4. **Tariff Taau (Wall Builder)** — *Heavy Grappler*: Massive grab damage, heavy overhead slams, Super: *200% Mega Tariff Wall Drop*.
5. **Dozer Baba (Saffron Monk)** — *Power Juggernaut*: Armored forward specials, high stun damage, Super: *Express Bulldozer Charge*.
6. **Expressway Nitin (Toll King)** — *Zoner / Spacer*: Long-reach poke attacks, Super: *8-Lane Flyover Drop*.
7. **Sutti Babu (File Master)** — *Technical Tactician*: Fast frame-trap jabs, Super: *Red Tape Bureaucracy Bind*.
8. **Rail Samrat (Chara King)** — *Trickster Heavyweight*: Deceptive crouch pokes and belly-bump knockback, Super: *Garib Rath Express Ram*.
9. **Chanakya Shah (Strategy Chief)** — *Defensive Mastermind*: Superior block stun recovery and punish throws, Super: *Chronology Samjhiye Checkmate*.
10. **Fact Bhai (YouTube Explainer)** — *Agile Footsie Fighter*: Quick backdash and long-range pointer pokes, Super: *Dosto Reality Check Beam*.
11. **Shanti Bapu (BOSS — Ahinsa Sage)** — *Secret Boss Archetype*: Reflects violent aggression; defeated either by enduring with **100% Ahinsa (Non-Violence)** or timing the **Budget Audit Strike (`B`)**.

## 3. Scoring, Progression & Balance
- **Match Rewards**:
  - Victory vs CPU: `+120 XP` (Easy), `+180 XP` (Normal), `+260 XP` (Hard), `+350 XP` (Boss Ahinsa Victory).
  - Online Multiplayer Victory: `+300 XP` and `+25 ELO Rating`.
  - Consolation (Completed Match Loss): `+45 XP` (prevents rage-quitting while rewarding participation).
- **Level Curve**: `Level = floor(sqrt(XP / 100)) + 1`, unlocking parliamentary titles (*Ward Parshad* → *Yuva Morcha Star* → *Cabinet Minister* → *Speaker of the House* → *Kursi Samrat*).
